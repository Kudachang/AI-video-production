# OVERDRIVE production rules

Apply these on every OVERDRIVE prompt. Use the seedance-director skill for prompt craft.

## Names
- In generation prompts: Punky (Vi), Akari (Akali), RoboRex / T-Hex, Mr. V (Viego). Never the LoL names in prompts.
- In public posts: VI and Akali are allowed (fan-made contest work). Never name AI tools or platforms in XHS posts; use generic wording. Say "a project", not "a pitch". Don't reveal unshot story beats.

## Locked asset facts
- RoboRex: two-story armored dinosaur-shaped machine, blue sensor eye, cannon inside the upper jaw (needed for the ending). NO machine gun on the right arm and NO missile launcher on the left arm. State this in every prompt, and crop them out of the reference if they appear.
- RoboRex damage: scuffs and light scratches early; hairline cracks and a slight dent on the skull after the hammer blows; never heavy damage before the jaw-rip.
- Punky: pink-red short hair, worn red leather jacket, two oversized brass-and-navy gauntlets, each about the size of her head. Always name both gauntlets, one on each hand. Gauntlets glow electric blue, never yellow ("warm glow" turns yellow).
- Gauntlet damage: both intact until RoboRex's tail strike; the left gauntlet cracks when she blocks the tail.
- Each gauntlet has exactly four knuckle plates. The locked double fist (hammer fist): thumbs up, knuckle columns vertical, bottom edge strikes. Use the double-fist reference only for double-fist shots; use the single gauntlet image for single-fist shots, or the model merges both hands into one gauntlet.
- Location: cave temple ruin with a fallen giant Buddha statue (cracked just above the feet), pagoda halls, a bell pavilion with a large bronze bell (3-4 m). Keep the bell on the same side of frame through the tail-hit sequence so her flight toward it matches.
- Lighting: dim cave, one shaft of cool daylight, cool desaturated tones, blue glow as the main accent. Desert scenes: flat hazy midday, never golden hour.

## Wording that passes the filters
- "Brings her down onto", "swings toward", "contact", "comes down onto" instead of smash, punch at, strike, slam.
- Hit the machine or the lens, never describe harm to a person. Dents, sparks and dust instead of gore.
- Repeated blows aimed at the camera get blocked; generate one strike and repeat it in the edit.
- A creature biting and lifting a person in one shot gets blocked; split it or hide the contact with camera angle.
- When a prompt is rejected, cut one element at a time to find the trigger: violent verbs, repetition, the face reference, the gauntlet reference.

## Audio line (always)
No BGM, no vocal, only SFX: then list the specific sounds.

## Platforms
- Lovart (Seedance 2.0/2.5) is the main tool. Higgsfield runs its own copyright filter plus Seedance's, so IP-heavy shots fail more there; use Higgsfield for gray-model blockouts, stills and non-IP references. MiniMax is last resort.
- Bill writes the prompts into Lovart himself: give paste-ready prompts, one clip at a time.

## Working style
- Keep prompts short and one action per clip; multi-action one-takes have failed repeatedly.
- When a shot fails twice, change structure (split, new reference, new angle) instead of adding words.
- Ask for the exact rejection text when a generation is blocked.
